Our Vision

To champion mathematical clarity in digital tools and untainted authenticity in foundational physical provisions. We build systems without redundant layers and source harvests without synthetic enhancement.

History

Established as a dual-horizon multidisciplinary project, KRA3T6 originated from a mandate to reject marketplace noise. What started as an internal set of calculation tools for commerce logistics evolved into a full studio practice spanning both digital auditing systems and direct botanical sourcing.

Services We Offer

Digital Systems & Audit Frameworks: We design custom unit-economic spreadsheets, fee-leakage detectors, and margin calculators tailored for e-commerce and marketplace operators.

Botanical & Pantry Allocations: We coordinate direct-from-harvest single-origin botanical sourcing, delivering small-batch, unpolished spices and provisions to discerning clients.

Research & Analysis: We generate independent operational reports, cost-architecture teardowns, and actionable insights to guide business strategy and risk mitigation.
